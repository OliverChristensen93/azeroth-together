AZEROTH TOGETHER
================

A co-op survivors game in World of Warcraft 1.12, for up to four players.


SETTING UP (once)
-----------------
This folder has the game, but not World of Warcraft's art and sound: you bring
those from your own copy of AzerothSurvivors (by Aethus).

1. Find your AzerothSurvivors folder. It holds AzerothSurvivors.exe and a
   folder called Data.
2. Copy that Data folder into this folder, next to azeroth-together.exe:

       AzerothTogether\
           azeroth-together.exe
           content\
           Data\          <- copied from AzerothSurvivors
           PLAYING.txt

3. Double-click azeroth-together.exe.

The game keeps its settings in a benilla-config folder it makes here.


PLAYING TOGETHER
----------------
One player hosts, the others join.

Host:
  - Pick a hero and a zone, then click Host Co-op.
  - The first time, Windows Firewall asks about the game: click Allow.
  - The panel shows an address like 192.168.0.103:7777. Send it to your friends.
  - When everyone is in, click Start Run.

Join:
  - Pick a hero, click Join Co-op, type the host's address, press Enter.

On the same home network, the address the panel shows works as it is.

Over the internet, the host must forward TCP port 7777 on their router to their
PC, and friends type the host's public IP address instead (search "what is my
IP" on the host's PC). Everyone needs the same version of the game.


IN A RUN
--------
WoW controls: WASD, right mouse to turn, Space to jump.
Level up: 1, 2, 3 or click a card; R rerolls.
A fallen friend comes back if you stand beside them for 3 seconds.
